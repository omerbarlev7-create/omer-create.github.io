## Tier 3 — Action (the sales bridge)

The `Ring` keyword column: `susanxtwo` · `johagorin` · `leeatoged` · `srleff` ·
`tamar_zionov` · `mitranath` — plus "Ring! Great Idea 💗"

Not applause. **Conversion.** A whole column of people typing a keyword because
the content asked them to.

This is the single most commercially persuasive asset in the set, and it gets
its own frame (6). The repetition is the message — do not crop it tight, the
visual rhythm of the repeated word is the point.
