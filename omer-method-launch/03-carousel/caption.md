## Why the caption is long

A pinned post is not a feed post. Its job is not reach — it is to explain the
whole thing to someone who just landed on the profile and is deciding whether to
follow or leave. That reader has already chosen to engage, so length costs
nothing and substance earns the follow.

The hook still has to survive the 125-character truncation, which is why the
question opens it and the admission lands immediately after.
