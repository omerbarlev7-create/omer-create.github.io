# OMER METHOD — Story Launch Sequence

**10 frames · 1080×1920 · Hebrew + English on every frame**
Goal: Proof → Authority → Sale · Destination: `omermethod.com`
Doubles as a permanent Highlight.

## The strategic angle

Not "I launched guides." Instead:
> *"For 3 years people asked me the wrong question. Here is the right answer."*

A product announcement loses people at frame 2. A gap keeps them to the end —
and the answer to the gap **is** the product.

**The through-line:** every reaction Omer ever got was about the story, not the
technology. That insight justifies the product and separates OMER AI from every
other AI account.

## Execution notes

| Topic | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Link** | Frames 9 + 10 in the live run. **In the Highlight, add one to frame 1 too** — some viewers open and leave after a single frame. |
| **Rhythm** | Frames 2, 7, 10 are type-only. They are the breathing room that holds pace. |
| **Video** | Frame 8 must be Omer speaking. If a second one exists, make it 7. People buy from faces. |
| **Comments** | Blur usernames, keep avatars and like counts. The numbers are the proof. |
| **Sticker** | Poll or emoji-slider on frame 5: *"Also thought it was the AI?"* — lifts engagement mid-sequence. |
| **Consistency** | Frame 1 hook and the landing page headline should feel like the same voice. |

## If cutting to 8

Merge 3+4 (Monica + the three worlds) into one proof frame, and merge 9+10.
**Never cut 5, 6 or 7** — they are the entire argument.
